# Bajra Bowl: pre-launch concept survey

We're gathering reactions to **Bajra Bowl**, an instant millet breakfast cup that
has not launched yet.

Read the product brief in `input/context.md`, then answer every question in the
questionnaire as yourself. We want your honest reaction to the price, the health
claim, and whether this fits how you actually eat breakfast, not feature ideas
outside the brief.

## How to answer

- Read the brief before you start.
- Answer every required question.
- For multiple-choice, use the listed option ids.
- For rating scales, use a whole number in the given range.
- Where a question asks for a short reason, keep it to one or two sentences and
  make it specific to your own situation.
- Write your answers to `/app/output/survey_result.json`.
