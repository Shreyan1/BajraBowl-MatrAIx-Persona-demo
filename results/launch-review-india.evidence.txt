## The product brief respondents read

# Bajra Bowl: product concept brief

**Category:** Packaged breakfast / better-for-you foods
**Market:** India
**Stage:** Pre-launch concept test (D2C launch planned next quarter)

## One-liner

Bajra Bowl is a 3-minute instant breakfast cup made from pearl millet (bajra),
sattu and roasted vegetables. A hot, filling Indian breakfast for mornings when
there is no time to cook.

## The product

- **Preparation:** tear the lid, add hot water to the line, stir, wait 3 minutes.
  No stove, no cooking, no washing up.
- **Three variants:** Masala Khichdi, Pudina Upma, Sweet Ragi Porridge
- **Per cup:** 12 g protein, 6 g fibre, no maida, no palm oil, no added sugar in
  the savoury variants
- **Claim on pack:** *"Low glycaemic index, diabetic-friendly breakfast in 3 minutes"*
- **Shelf life:** 9 months, no refrigeration

## Pricing (proposed)

- **Single cup:** ₹79
- **Monthly pack (30 cups):** ₹1,899, which works out to ₹63 per cup
- **First-time trial pack (4 cups):** ₹199

For reference, the same breakfast cooked at home from loose ingredients costs
roughly ₹15 to ₹25 per serving and takes about 20 minutes.

## Where you would buy it

- Own website, with an optional monthly subscription
- Quick-commerce apps (10-minute delivery)
- Not planned for kirana or general trade at launch

## Who we think it is for

Working adults aged 25 to 45 in cities who skip breakfast or eat something fried on
the way to work, and adults managing blood sugar who have been told to switch to
millets but find them inconvenient to cook.

## What this survey is probing

- Reaction to the ₹79 single-cup price against a ₹15 to ₹25 home-cooked equivalent
- Whether the "diabetic-friendly" claim widens the appeal or narrows it
- Whether an instant cup competes credibly with home-cooked breakfast
- Whether this is bought for yourself or for someone else in the family
- Which sales channel feels natural
- What would actually trigger a first purchase
