# Launch review: Bajra Bowl

**Do not launch yet**

The product has a strong health hook but a fatal pricing gap; 85% of respondents cannot justify the cost over home-cooked alternatives.

Written by `gemma4:31b-cloud` from 7 survey responses across `bajra-gemma4cloud-n8`. The numbers are extracted from the job output. The judgement is the model's, and it is worth exactly as much as you think a small sample and a local model are worth.

## Where it fails

### Price-to-value proposition is non-existent (high severity)

The cost of ₹79 is perceived as too high compared to the ₹15-25 home-cooked cost, leading to a total lack of purchase intent.

*Evidence:* 6 out of 7 respondents chose 'cannot_justify_the_gap' for homemade substitution; 6 out of 7 said 'false' to buying at launch.

### Price point exceeds budget thresholds (high severity)

The current pricing is viewed as a barrier rather than a premium for convenience.

*Evidence:* 3 respondents said the price is 'too_expensive_walk_away' and 3 said they would only buy 'only_on_offer'.

## What is working

- **Strong resonance with health/diabetic claims** 5 out of 7 respondents stated the 'diabetic-friendly' claim draws them in.
- **High interest in low-friction trial** 5 out of 7 respondents identified the ₹199 trial pack as the primary trigger for purchase.

## Where it could still win

### Caregivers for elderly family members

More respondents are interested in buying this for an elder than for themselves.

*Do this:* Pivot marketing from 'busy professional' to 'health-conscious caregiving' and test a lower price point.

## Before you launch

1. **Drastically reduce the single-cup price or introduce a 'value' version.** 6 out of 7 respondents explicitly stated that paying 3x more than home-cooked food is not feasible.
2. **Shift the primary target persona from 'working adults' to 'caregivers for diabetic elders'.** 4 out of 7 respondents indicated they would buy this for an elder family member, compared to only 2 for themselves.
3. **Prioritize Quick-Commerce over D2C website for launch.** Quick-commerce was the preferred channel for 4 out of 7 respondents.

## What this survey cannot tell you

- Taste preference (only 1 respondent mentioned taste as a primary driver).
- Willingness to pay for the monthly subscription specifically.
- Actual conversion rate of the ₹199 trial pack into repeat purchases.
